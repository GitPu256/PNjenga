### About Me

I'm building practical, production-minded AI systems for **Security Operations Centers**. My work focuses on solving real operational pain points — high false-positive rates, black-box models, and the lack of proper human oversight in AI-driven security tools.

I design hybrid detection pipelines, make model decisions transparent with explainable AI, and embed strong governance so analysts stay in control.

**What I care about:**
- Reducing alert fatigue with smarter, contextual detection
- Making AI decisions understandable (SHAP + LIME)
- Mapping detections to MITRE ATT&CK
- Building layered, maintainable architectures
- Responsible AI practices and human-in-the-loop design
